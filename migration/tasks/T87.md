# T87. Contact form: visible labels, spacing, photo and submit button

## Goal

The contact card is comfortable to fill in and matches the rest of the system.

## Context

Visual audit of staging, 2026-11-13 (`/#kontakt`, `/kontakt`, and the contact section on service pages).

- Fields show placeholders only ("E-mail", "Telefon", "Temat rozmowy"). DESIGN.md: a visible label above the field, Body S 500, with a required marker. The placeholder is never the only label.
- Fields are 12 px apart, and the help text, consent checkbox and submit follow at the same tight spacing. The group has no rhythm.
- The consent checkbox is 20 × 20 px (touch target below 44 px).
- The submit button is weight 400 with a 50 px radius, unlike every other button (see T86).
- The photo in the card is a square with no radius inside a 20 px card. At 390 px it becomes a 60 px thumbnail floating alone above "Bezpośredni kontakt".
- "Skontaktuj się ze mną" and its subtitle are centred, while every other section intro is left-aligned. The card content starts at 184 px from the left at 1440 px, while the page content starts at 120 px.

## Requirements

- Visible labels above each field, required marker, placeholders as hints only. Error state per DESIGN.md (Error Red stroke and a Body S message under the field).
- Spacing from the scale with a clear order: label → field tight, field → field medium, fields → legal/consent → submit larger.
- The checkbox keeps its visual size but its clickable row (box + label) is at least 44 px tall.
- The submit button uses the shared button from T86 (same height, weight, radius, arrow).
- Photo: 6 px radius (a part inside a surface) or a circle avatar, one consistent treatment at all widths. At 390 px it sits beside the "Bezpośredni kontakt" heading or is hidden. No orphan thumbnail.
- Section intro left-aligned and on the same left edge as other sections. The card may keep its inner padding.
- **Form invariants stay untouched:** the POST target, all 32 hidden attribution fields, Turnstile, field `name` attributes, the wait label and the success/error flow. Compare the submitted payload before and after against a local stub, never the production webhook.
- The same `LeadForm` drives the newsletter form. Check it on the navy band and on `/newsletter` after the change.
- Use the `impeccable` and `good-css` skills.

## Done when

- [ ] Before/after screenshots of the contact card at 390, 768 and 1440 px, and of the newsletter form at 390 and 1440 px.
- [ ] Payload diff (field names and count) before vs after, posted to a local stub: identical.
- [ ] Keyboard pass: tab order, visible focus ring on every control, error message announced (`aria-describedby` or equivalent).
- [ ] Checkbox row measured ≥ 44 px tall at 390 px.
- [ ] `pnpm test`, the build and the e2e suite pass.
