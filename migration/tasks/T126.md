# T126. Apply the reviewed T51 SEO shortening to production

## Goal

Studio blocks publishing in 30 production documents. On 2026-11-20, `sanity documents validate --dataset production` reported 44 errors, all SEO max-length markers: 25 titles over 60 characters (23 posts, `page-63dfea350c95fc855e2647ed`, `siteSettings-pl.defaultSeo`) and 19 post descriptions over 160. Every value is a verbatim copy of the live Webflow page. Until they are shortened, an editor cannot publish any fix in those documents without rewriting the SEO fields first.

T51 kept the limits by the owner's decision and prepared reviewed shorter copy in `migration/lanes/cms/out/t51-validation/seo-review.tsv`, but its patch was never applied and no task tracked the apply. All 44 current overflows are rows in that review. The other 10 rows were fixed by hand since T51 and must not be touched.

## Requirements

1. Take a fresh backup with `node migration/tools/backup-sanity.mjs` before any write. Record the archive path (outside git) and counts in Handoff.
2. Export the published SEO projection from the T51 README. Filter `seo-review.tsv` to rows whose `old_text` still equals the current value, and run `migration/tools/import/seo-t51-dry-run.py` unchanged against that filtered review. Keep the filtered review and the generated patch in `migration/lanes/cms/out/t126-seo-apply/`. Keep the raw export outside the repo.
3. Do not change the schema limits or the reviewed copy. A field whose current text no longer matches the review is skipped and listed in Handoff.
4. Apply the `ifRevisionID`-guarded patch to `production` only after the owner's explicit yes at the moment of the write.
5. Rerun `sanity documents validate --dataset production` and record before and after marker counts.

## Done when

- [ ] Backup archive path and counts recorded in Handoff.
- [ ] Filtered review and generated patch committed under `migration/lanes/cms/out/t126-seo-apply/`.
- [ ] Owner's approval recorded, patch applied, every revision guard matched.
- [ ] Production validation shows 0 SEO max-length markers and no new markers.
- [ ] `tasks.py check` passes.
