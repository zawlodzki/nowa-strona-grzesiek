# T33. Keep the hero image badge attached to the photo on mid and mobile widths

## Goal

On mid-width and mobile viewports the optional hero photo badge (added in T32) detaches from the photo and jumps to the left corner. Fix this in the shared `Hero.astro` component so every page that has a CMS badge keeps it overlaid on the photo, the way live does. Design scope is refine, not redesign: when in doubt, match live.

## Inputs

- Live pages: `https://zawlodzki.pl/sales-atlas-system` (standard badge, Webflow `.badge-naroznik` inside `.foto-hero-wrapper{position:relative}`) and `https://zawlodzki.pl/szkolenie-i-wdrozenie-salesforce` (wide Salesforce badge).
- Staging: `https://zawlodzki-web.zawlodzki.workers.dev/sales-atlas-system` and `/szkolenie-i-wdrozenie-salesforce`.
- Shared component: `apps/web/src/components/blocks/Hero.astro` (badge styles live in the component; there is no separate CSS file). The badge is `.image-badge`, absolutely positioned; the two-column layout switches on `@container (width >= 48rem)` while badge offsets switch on `@media (max-width: 991px)`.
- `DESIGN.md`, `.claude/skills/good-css/SKILL.md`, `.claude/skills/impeccable/SKILL.md`, `.claude/skills/verify-web`.

## Steps

1. Diagnose why the badge leaves the photo on mid widths (~768–1024) and mobile (375). Start with positioning: the badge must be positioned against the photo box, not a larger hero or column container, at every breakpoint. Check container vs viewport breakpoints, whether `.portrait` shrinks to the image, and whether negative offsets or stacking let the badge escape.
2. Fix only `Hero.astro`. Do not add route-specific overrides. Pages without a CMS `badgeImage` must still render no badge and no empty space. Keep existing `badgeSize` (`standard` and `wide`) sizes, the hero CTA, and desktop 1280 behaviour unless a small shared tweak is required to keep the badge on the photo.
3. Prove with verify-web (`.claude/skills/verify-web`, `scripts/web.sh up|probe|shot|down`, port 8790). Side-by-side live vs local screenshots of the hero for `/sales-atlas-system` and `/szkolenie-i-wdrozenie-salesforce` at 1280, 1024, 768 and 375, plus one page without a badge as a control (hero only, no empty gap). Save everything under `migration/lanes/design/out/hero-badge-responsive/` with an `index.md` mapping each shot. End with `scripts/web.sh down`.
4. No Sanity schema or dataset writes, no deploy, no merge.

## Done when

- [ ] The hero badge stays attached to the photo like live at 1280, ~768–1024 and 375, for both the standard badge (`/sales-atlas-system`) and the wide badge (`/szkolenie-i-wdrozenie-salesforce`).
- [ ] Side-by-side verify-web comparisons (live vs local `wrangler dev`, port 8790) for those two pages at 1280, 1024, 768 and 375, plus a no-badge control page, are in `migration/lanes/design/out/hero-badge-responsive/` and listed in `index.md`.
- [ ] A page with no CMS badge still has no badge and no empty space.
- [ ] No horizontal overflow at 375 (`documentElement.scrollWidth == 375`) on the two badged pages.
- [ ] `astro check`, `node --test src` and `pnpm build` pass in `apps/web`, and `python3 -I migration/tools/tasks.py check` passes.
- [ ] Handoff states the cause, the fix, and any remaining intended differences from live.
