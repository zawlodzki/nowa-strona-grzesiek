# T46. Contact: phone format and link underlines

## Goal

On the original Webflow site, the phone in the contact block is shown as `+48 571 088 338`, and the email and phone links are not underlined. Ours differs: landing contact details (home and the service pages that reuse the same block) print the person document's unformatted E.164 value, and the email/phone links pick up the default underline.

Current production content (read-only check 2026-11-11):

- `person.telephone` is `+48571088338`. `Sections.astro` passes that string through to `ContactForm`. Landing variants with `showDetails: true` and no channels (home, plus most product/service pages) render `{contact.telephone}` as-is. The `tel:` href already strips to E.164 via `contact.telephone?.replace(/[^\d+]/g, '')` and must stay `tel:+48571088338`.
- `/kontakt` (full variant with channels) and `/o-mnie` (channels variant) already store the display label as `+48 571 088 338` with `href` `tel:+48571088338`. The format bug is not in those labels; underlines still apply to those links.
- `siteSettings.telephone` is empty. JSON-LD/entity telephone should stay E.164.

1. Prefer rendering-side formatting in `ContactForm.astro` when the stored value is digits or E.164: display `+48 571 088 338`, keep `tel:+48571088338`. Do not change the stored `person.telephone` just to fix the label. If a production Sanity write ever looks necessary, do not apply it; deliver an `ifRevisionID`-guarded dry-run script as `migration/tools/import/pages-t46*` plus a readable change list in `migration/lanes/design/out/t46-contact/`, and stop for Grzesiek's "tak".
2. Email and phone links in the contact block (details column and channel rows on `/kontakt` and the home contact block) must have no underline, with a visible hover and focus state. No cyan. Atlas Blue `#3a48cc` is allowed. Do not change global `a` styles in `base.css` / `layout.css`; keep the rule scoped to these contact links. Match live Webflow if Signal/WhatsApp in the same channel list are also un-underlined.
3. Touch only owned files. If a design-system fixture (`apps/web/src/pages/_ds/blocks-b.astro`) or a small shared formatter/test is required, add it to `owns` in the PR and say why. Do not edit SEO snapshots or entity telephone just to pretty-print the visible label.
