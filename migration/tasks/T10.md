## Handoff

**What changed.**
- `components/blocks/Pricing.astro`, `Testimonials.astro`, `Faq.astro`, `ContactForm.astro`, `NewsletterSignup.astro` and `RichText.astro` render the six blocks. Each takes `data` typed from `apps/studio/sanity.types.ts`. None of them fetch anything. Resolved references come in as props: `Testimonials` takes `items: ResolvedTestimonial[]` and keeps the order of `data.items`, skipping unresolved refs. `ContactForm` takes `contact: { email, telephone, image }` from the person document. `Faq` and `RichText` take `links` (document `_ref` to URL) and `images` (asset `_ref` to `ResolvedImage`).
- `components/portable-text/` is the Portable Text renderer shared by posts and `richText`. It renders the four text styles, nested bullet and number lists, the five marks, and the `image`, `table`, `codeBlock`, `embed`, `download` and `callout` objects. Wide tables scroll inside their frame with a fade on the edge that still has content. Embeds accept only https. YouTube goes through `youtube-nocookie.com`. An internal link or image without a resolved entry fails the build with the key named, so nothing renders a dead link.
- `components/blocks/faq.ts` holds `visibleFaqItems` and `emitsFaqPage`. `Faq.astro` renders exactly `visibleFaqItems(data)` (questions without an answer are dropped). It marks its section `data-faq-schema="FAQPage"` and `id="faq"` when `emitsFaqPage(data)` is true. **T12 should call `emitsFaqPage` on the page's first faq section** rather than re-deriving visibility.
- `components/forms/LeadForm.astro` is the shared form shell for both forms. It posts to `/api/form?form=<key>`, sets `data-name` to the Webflow form name, renders the Turnstile widget, and renders every field of `FORMS[key]` from `worker/form.ts` that the slot does not render as a hidden input, with `data-zaw-attribution="<name>"` inside `div[data-zaw-attribution-fields="v1"]` (same markup as the live site, so the c15t/attribution filler finds them). The Worker's field table is therefore the single source of names. A visible field name the Worker doesn't know fails the build. Without JavaScript the form posts natively and the Worker's 303 lands on the thank-you page. With it, `fetch` follows the 303 or shows the Worker's message in a live region.
- `migration/tools/check-form-fields.mjs` is the script check. It matches each `<form>` by `data-name` to `forms.json` (ignoring Webflow style-guide demo forms) and fails on any missing, extra or duplicated name.
- `pages/_ds/blocks-b.astro` is the noindex catalog with typical and worst-case data for each block. `pages/[ds]/blocks-b.astro` serves it at `/_ds/blocks-b`, the same wrapper T11 uses, because Astro skips underscore folders.
- `.claude/skills/verify-web/features/forms.md` is the forms recipe T14 asked for, linked from the features README.
