# T88. Logo strips and the article shell line up with the page

## Goal

Remove the two places where the page's left edge and alignment visibly jump, and shorten the logo wall on phones.

## Context

Visual audit of staging, 2026-11-13.

- **Logo strip.** "Firmy które korzystają już z mojego doświadczenia" and "Łączę narzędzia, z których zespół już korzysta" are centred, between left-aligned sections above and below. DESIGN.md: hero and section text is left-aligned at every width.
- **Phone.** At 390 px the 24 client logos stack in two columns and take about 1060 px right after the hero. The tools strip adds about 400 px. DESIGN.md calls the 24-logo wall a template habit to quiet.
- **Optical size.** Logos render at very different visual weights in the same grid (for example "rentli" and "pipedrive" large, "iab" and "ANTHROPIC" tiny).
- **Blog post.** At 1440 px the article shell (breadcrumbs, H1, TOC) starts 64 px from the left, while the header logo and every marketing page start at 120 px.

## Requirements

- Logo strip heading and intro left-aligned, on the container's left edge. The grid starts on the same edge.
- At < 768 px the client logos take at most about one viewport height: fewer columns of smaller logos, a horizontal scroll-snap row, or a capped number with the rest behind a disclosure. Pick one, justify it in Handoff. No auto-playing marquee. All logos stay in the DOM with their alt text.
- Normalise optical size: each logo fits a box with a shared height and a max width so visual weights are comparable. Respect the existing `invert` handling.
- Article shell: its outer edges align with the site container (same left edge as the header logo at 1440 px), while the DESIGN.md blog layout holds (760 px column, 200 px TOC left and CTA rail right at ≥ 1280 px). If both cannot hold at 1280–1440 px, keep the column centred and document the trade-off.
- Do not change `Testimonials.astro` (T81).
- Use the `impeccable` and `good-css` skills.
