# T103. AI sales audit page diagrams in the calm scene style (problem, pre-audit and steps 1–4)

## Goal

This is the next subpage after the CRM audit. `/audyt-ai-sprzedaz` has six scenes. Rebuild all of them in the calm style. `zw-problem` also appears on `/kurs-ai-w-sprzedazy`, `/program-ai-dla-zespolu-sprzedazy` and `/wdrozenie-ai-w-sprzedazy-b2b`.

## Context

- This task builds on T96–T102 (PRs #107, #108, #109, #112, #114, #115, #116), all in review. It uses `ListScene.astro` (T100) and `sequence.ts` (T97). The branch starts from the T102 branch, so those PRs need to be merged first.
- Page copy:
  - Problem: the costliest mistake is putting AI where it makes no sense. The AI programme page quotes McKinsey, State of AI 2025: 88% of organisations use AI regularly, but only 39% report any EBIT impact.
  - Before the audit: a survey and the basic data (sales stages, tools, a sample of reports, the list of repetitive tasks).
  - Step 1: map the sales process and mark where the team loses time, waits for data or retypes information.
  - Step 2: data, CRM and tools, plus the risks around data quality, GDPR and access.
  - Step 3: score each use case for business value, data availability, effort and risk. The result is a short list of 3–5 processes to start with, and a "not now" list.
  - Step 4: the plan and the decision: first process, tools, scope, owners, metrics, a 90-day plan and a go/no-go.

## Requirements

1. Rebuild the five audit scenes on `ListScene`. `zw-problem` gets its own scene on `sequence.ts`, with the two McKinsey figures drawn to scale and the source named. It must read correctly on all four pages that use it.
2. No new scene scripts. Follow the direction recorded in T92 and carry the T96 header marker.
3. Everything except the McKinsey figures is a placeholder until Grzesiek confirms it.
