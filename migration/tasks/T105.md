# T105. AI programme, implementation and course diagrams in the calm scene style (steps 1–5, next step)

## Goal

These are the last scenes in the old style. `zw-step1`–`zw-step5` appear on `/program-ai-dla-zespolu-sprzedazy` and `/wdrozenie-ai-w-sprzedazy-b2b`. `zw-step1` is also on `/kurs-ai-w-sprzedazy`, next to `zw-course-next`. Rebuild all six so that every scene on the site uses the calm style.

## Context

- This task builds on T96–T104 (PRs #107 to #118), all in review. It uses `ListScene.astro` (T100), `SwitchScene.astro` (T98) and `sequence.ts` (T97). Its branch starts from the T104 branch, so merge those PRs first.
- The steps mean the same thing on all the pages that use them:
  1. Choose the repetitive work AI takes over (offers, follow-ups, briefs, reports, forecasts), with the time it costs counted.
  2. Build the company context: offer, pricing, client profile, tone, objections.
  3. Connect tools: CRM, mail, calendar, documents and meeting transcripts, read-only at the start.
  4. First agent: one workflow closed end to end. The programme's target is 9 of 10 agent actions without a manual fix.
  5. Scale up: further agents, plus a person who runs it after the programme.
- `zw-course-next`: the course is the cheapest way in. If the buyer moves to the group programme or a dedicated implementation, the course price is deducted.

## Requirements

1. Rebuild the six scenes on `ListScene`, `SwitchScene` and `sequence.ts`. Step 4 gets its own to-scale bars for the share of actions done without a fix. No new scene scripts. Follow the direction recorded in T92 and carry the T96 header marker.
2. The scenes must read correctly on every page that uses them.
3. The figures are placeholders until Grzesiek confirms them. The only exception is the 9-of-10 target, which comes from the page copy.
