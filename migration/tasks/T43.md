## Handoff

Implemented in shared `Tabs.astro`; three visual alternatives in `_ds/blocks-a.astro`.

Root cause: the normal-flow empty `.runway` added N scroll slices after the stage, although N layers need N−1 transitions. It reserved 2700px at 1440×1000, making the whole tabs block 3535px. Replaced that empty sibling with an explicit sticky containing-block minimum of measured stage + (N−1) steps. Now 3235px (835px stage + 2400px travel). The stage occupies that travel while pinned; the remaining height is required for scroll switching, not a blank section to remove. Layer selection rounds to the nearest stop; clicks and keyboard selection align to the same stops. Removed the 24px fit tolerance and remeasure after fonts load. At short desktop heights, mobile, and reduced motion, no scroll reserve applies. No production content writes.
