Grzesiek asked to continue with the remaining subpages. `/audyt-sprzedazy` has six scenes: the problem and one scene for each of the five decision areas of the audit. Rebuild them in the calm style.

- Page copy:
  - Problem: everyone sees a different cause. Marketing says it delivers leads, sales says the leads are weak, the manager wants more activity, and the owner does not know where the problem is.
  - Area 1, strategy and target maths: do the needed deals, conversion, deal value and team capacity add up to a real plan?

  - Area 5, CRM, data and measurement: can the data give conversion, cycle length, sources, pipeline value, loss reasons and forecast quality?
