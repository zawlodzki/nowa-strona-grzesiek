# T36. Tabs block: sticky panel and scroll-driven layers

## Goal

Grzesiek's feedback on https://zawlodzki-web.zawlodzki.workers.dev/sales-atlas-system (layer tabs section):
1. The panel with layer descriptions must be stuck to the top (top-aligned, sticky), not vertically centred; it must not move when switching layers.
2. While the page scrolls through the section, layers must switch automatically one after another (scroll-driven), while clicking a tab still works.

The change lives in the shared Tabs block, so it applies on every page that uses it.

## Done when

- [ ] Panel is top-aligned and sticky on desktop; its top edge does not move when switching any layer (prove with screenshots of at least 2 layers at the same scroll offset).
- [ ] Scrolling through the section advances the active layer in order and back on scroll up; clicking a tab still works and keeps scroll/state consistent.
- [ ] Mobile 375 and mid widths stay usable (no broken sticky, no scroll trap); respects prefers-reduced-motion.
- [ ] Keyboard and ARIA tab semantics still correct.
- [ ] verify-web evidence: screenshots live vs new at 1440 / 768 / 375 plus a scroll sequence showing auto-switching.
- [ ] `tasks.py check` passes; CI green.
