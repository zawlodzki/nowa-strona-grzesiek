# T129. Blog post rails fit their content

## Goal

Owner request, 2026-11-26, after an `impeccable audit` of https://www.zawlodzki.pl/blog/asana-vs-jira: the CTA button in the right rail of a blog post renders oddly. Fix the rail and the rest of the audit's findings on the post template.

## Context

Measured on production at 1280×900 before the change:

- At `min-width: 80rem` the post grid was `minmax(0, 1fr) minmax(0, 47.5rem) minmax(0, 1fr)` inside a container capped at 78rem. Both rails were 193 px at every viewport width, 1920 included.
- The sidebar CTA had 145 px inside its padding. "Umów konsultację" wrapped to two lines in a 145×74 pill with the arrow beside the first line. `--action-padding-inline: var(--space-2xs)` squeezed it further.
- The CTA text (`post.cta.label`, 93 characters on this post) was set as an Erode `type-heading-xs` H2: 7 lines, 229 px.
- The recommendations panel was 790 px, so the script switched it to `position: static` (`data-page-scroll`). The "sticky" CTA scrolled away with the page.
- The TOC panel was 798 px in a 900 px window at `inset-block-start: 7rem`. Its end and the share button fell below the fold, and the script fallback did not cover it.
- The CTA text was an H2 identical to the CTA band's H2 lower down the page.
- No skip link anywhere on the site. Breadcrumb links were 20 px tall.
- `main` re-declared `--text-link` and `--surface-accent` as `oklch(from #3a48cc l c h)`, the same value as the tokens, and `--text-link-active` as the same color.

An audit note about the "Udostępnij artykuł" H2 coming before the content was a false positive: it sits in a closed popover and is not exposed.

## Requirements

- At `min-width: 80rem` the right rail is wide enough for the CTA button on one line. The prose column gives up the difference and stays readable.
- The sidebar CTA text is body copy, not a heading, sized for a narrow rail.
- Sticky panels never exceed the window. A long panel scrolls inside itself instead of losing `position: sticky`; the CTA and the share button stay in view.
- When the TOC scrolls inside its column, the current entry stays visible.
- A skip link on every page, targeting `main#tresc`.
- Breadcrumb links at least 24 px tall.
- No hard-coded brand color in `PostView.astro`.

## Done when

- [x] At 1280×900 the CTA button is one line (216×52) and the CTA panel stays sticky.
- [x] At 1440×700 the CTA stays at the top of the rail and the related list scrolls inside the panel.
- [x] The TOC ends above the window bottom with the share button visible, and follows the current section.
- [x] 1024 and 375 px unchanged apart from the skip link and breadcrumb height; no horizontal scroll at 375.
- [x] `pnpm --filter web check` 0 errors, `pnpm --filter web test` passes.

## Handoff

**What changed**

- `PostView.astro`
  - Grid at `min-width: 80rem`: `11.25rem minmax(0, 47.5rem) 16.5rem`, gap `--space-l`. At 1280 px: TOC 180, prose 694, rail 264 (before: 193 / 760 / 193). The 78rem container caps the post at about 1200 px at every width, so the prose stays 692–694 px on wider screens.
  - Sidebar CTA text: `<h2 class="type-heading-xs">` became `<p class="sidebar-cta-text">` (Satoshi 17 px, semibold). This removes the duplicate H2 and takes the card from 375 to 239 px.
  - Sidebar button: full width, `body-s` size, `--action-padding-inline: var(--space-s)`.
  - Both sticky panels: `max-block-size: calc(100dvb - 7rem - var(--space-m))`. The TOC nav and the related-posts list scroll inside the panel with `overscroll-behavior: contain`. The `data-page-scroll` script and its CSS are removed.
  - `main` keeps `--text-link-active: var(--text-link)`, the behavior T48 set, without the hard-coded color.
  - Breadcrumb links get `padding-block: var(--space-3xs)`, for a height of 28 px.
- `PostToc.astro`: when the outline overflows, the active entry is scrolled into the nav, a third of the way down.
- `Base.astro` and `base.css`: a skip link "Przejdź do treści" / "Skip to content" is shown on focus. `main[id]` shares the header scroll margin with `section[id]`.
- `RouteView.astro`, `BlogIndexView.astro`, `index.astro`, `404.astro`: `<main id="tresc">`.

**Evidence** (local `astro dev`, production dataset, 2026-11-26)

- 1280×900: columns `180px 694px 264px`, button 216×52 with one label line, recommendations panel 598 px and sticky, share button bottom at 872 px when stuck.
- 1440×700: panel 112–676 px, CTA top at 116 px, related list scrolls (324 px of content in 285 px). The TOC's current entry stays in view at 30, 60 and 90% of the article (nav `scrollTop` 0 → 233).
- 1024×800: two columns (170 / 757), sidebar CTA hidden as before, TOC within the window.
- 375×812: `scrollWidth` 375, TOC collapsed to 44 px, breadcrumb link 28 px.
- Keyboard: the first Tab from the top shows the skip link at the top left.
- Console: only the expected c15t CORS errors from a localhost origin.
