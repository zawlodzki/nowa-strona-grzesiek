# T96. Remaining homepage diagrams in the calm scene style (automation, Asana) and a one-row funnel

## Goal

T92 rebuilt three of the five homepage scenes. Grzesiek asked for the other two, `zw-automation` and `zw-asana`, in the same style. He also flagged that the CRM funnel must stay one row at every width: a pipeline reads left to right, so stages may not stack under each other (T92 put them in a 2×2 grid under 30rem).

## Requirements

1. `crm-funnel`: the four stages stay in one row at every width, down to the 320px slot. No stage under another.
2. Rebuild `zw-automation` and `zw-asana` on the T92 scene base (`scene/scene.css`, `scene/timeline.ts`). They follow the approved direction recorded in T92: hairlines and type, Polish labels, site tokens only, calm tempo, one moving thing at a time, end state in the markup, one closing number.
3. `zw-automation` answers `zw-manual-relay` (the same track language, but connected): data passes between systems without manual copying.
4. `zw-asana` shows the Asana hierarchy (goal, project, tasks with an owner and a due date) and work rolling up into the goal.
5. Move the step track shared by the relay and the automation scene into `scene.css` instead of copying it.
6. Keep `port-diagrams.mjs` from writing the rebuilt files. Example names, dates and times are placeholders until Grzesiek confirms them.

## Done when

- [x] The funnel is one row of four stages in the wide slot and the 320px slot of `/_ds/diagrams` and on the homepage at 375px.
- [x] `zw-automation` and `zw-asana` render without overflow in both slots, play once on entering the viewport and show the full end state without JavaScript.
- [x] No colour outside the site tokens; no text in Signal Cyan.
- [x] `tasks.py check`, `pnpm --dir apps/web check` and the build pass. PR opened with status `review`. Do not merge.

## Handoff

### What changed

- `CrmFunnel.astro`: the 2×2 layout under 30rem is gone. The four stages stay in one row at every width. In narrow containers the count moves under the stage name and the column gap shrinks to 0.5rem. "Kwalifikacja" carries a soft hyphen as a fallback, but it fits whole at 320px and 375px.
- `scene/scene.css`: the shared step track (`sc-track`, `sc-step`, `sc-ring`, `sc-step-name`, `sc-step-what`, `sc-link`, `sc-link-fill`, `sc-link-label`). A link's line now runs from ring centre to ring centre (`--sc-step / -2`), so it meets the rings at every step width. T92 used a fixed −1.6rem, which left small gaps in wide slots.
- `ZwManualRelay.astro`: moved onto the shared track. It only sets a dashed line, Error Red on slow links and the carrier dot. It looks and plays the same as before.
- `ZwAutomation.astro` (new): the counterpart to the relay. The same track is drawn with solid lines: Formularz → CRM → E-mail → Kalendarz. A Signal Cyan fill runs along each link in 900ms, the next ring fills Atlas Blue and the step time appears. It closes on "Od formularza do spotkania · 7 s". It is deliberately quicker than the relay (whole scene about 5s) to show the contrast between automation and manual work.
- `ZwAsana.astro` (new): an outline of a goal, a project under it and three tasks, each with an owner's initials and a due date. Tasks are ticked one by one, the project bar grows by a third each time and "Postęp projektu" counts up to 100%. Dates hide under 26rem.
- `port/port-diagrams.mjs`: `ZwAutomation.astro` and `ZwAsana.astro` were added to the rebuilt set. `--check` against the inventory gives `52 diagrams, 0 file(s) stale, 0 unsized`.
