# T83. Pricing cards show one currency unit and stay aligned

## Goal

Pricing cards read correctly and their rows line up across the three plans.

## Context

Visual audit of staging, 2026-11-13, `/szkolenie-i-wdrozenie-pipedrive#cennik`:

- The price renders as "3 900 zł zł netto" and "6 000 zł zł netto / mies.". `Pricing.astro` prints `amount + currency` and then `plan.unit`, and the content's `unit` already starts with "zł".
- The third plan has no price. Its label "Wycena indywidualna" renders at the Price size (55 px), wraps to two lines, and pushes its description and button below the neighbours. Its button label "Porozmawiajmy o zakresie i cenie" wraps to two lines.
- The impeccable detector flags `#wdrozenie-5-handlowcow` (the tier tag) for cramped padding: 5 px above and below 17 px text.

Other pricing sections (AI, Asana, Salesforce, audits, course) were not checked. Check every page that renders `.pricing`.

## Requirements

- The currency appears once, whatever the content holds. Make the component robust (do not print the currency when the unit already begins with it, or the reverse). Do not write to Sanity in this task. If a content cleanup is still wanted, list the affected plans in Handoff.
- A plan without a numeric price uses a smaller type role (Title, not Price) so it fits one line at 1440 px, and the header area of all cards keeps a common baseline: tag, price row, note, description and button align across cards at ≥ 992 px (subgrid, as DESIGN.md asks).
- Buttons in the cards never wrap to two lines at 1440 px. At 390 px they may be full width.
- Tier tag padding follows DESIGN.md (`0 × 8px`, Label size) without the text touching the edge.
- Prices and plan content are taken from Sanity as they are. Do not invent or change any price.
- Follow the `good-css` skill.

## Done when

- [x] No page renders a doubled currency. List the pages checked and paste the rendered price strings in Handoff.
- [x] Screenshots of the Pipedrive pricing section at 390, 768 and 1440 px show aligned rows and single-line buttons on desktop.
- [x] A test covers the price string for: amount + unit starting with the currency, amount + unit without it, and no amount.
- [x] `pnpm test` and the build pass.
