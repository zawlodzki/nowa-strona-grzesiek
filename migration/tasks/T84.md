# T84. Section rhythm, grouping and heading hierarchy

## Goal

Related sections read as one group and unrelated sections are clearly separated. Today every section has the same spacing and the same heading size, so nothing leads.

## Context

Visual audit of staging, 2026-11-13.

- Every section is `padding-block: 64px` (48 px at 390) on a white background: 15 sections on home, 20 on `/szkolenie-i-wdrozenie-pipedrive`. Any two neighbours are 128 px apart.
- An intro and its own content are split like strangers. `/sales-atlas-system`: "Dziewięć warstw" intro and the tabs below it are two sections, 168 px apart. Pipedrive: "Od audytu procesu do zespołu…" and the five steps (KROK 1–5). Home: "Zacznijmy od miejsca, które dziś najbardziej blokuje pracę zespołu" and the four service rows (#crm, #ai, #automatyzacja, #asana).
- All of those child rows use an H2 at 45 px, the same as the intro above them. Steps and services under a shared intro should sit one level down (DESIGN.md: Title, 28 → 35 px, for steps and sub-sections).
- On mobile a scene sits at the same distance from its own text as from the next heading, so it is unclear which block it belongs to.
- Only one or two tonal bands per page break the white (pricing on Mist, the navy bands).

T81 owns `Sections.astro` until it merges, hence the dependency.

## Requirements

- Keep DESIGN.md's 64 px (48 px at 390) rhythm between unrelated sections. Inside a group, spacing is clearly tighter (pick tokens from the existing scale, no one-off values), so the squint test shows groups.
- Decide grouping from the data, not from page-specific hacks: a Feature or Tabs section that directly follows an intro-only ContentSection, and consecutive Features that carry a step label (KROK n) or follow such an intro, form a group. Describe the rule you chose in Handoff. Do not change the Sanity schema. If a clean rule needs a schema field, stop and write a `Contract request`.
- Grouped children render their heading at the Title role and at the next heading level (H3 under the group's H2). The document outline must stay valid: no skipped levels.
- On mobile, a scene or image stays visually attached to its own text (closer to it than to the next block).
- Do not touch `apps/web/src/components/diagrams/**`. Scenes are handled by a separate agent.
- Do not change URLs, ids used as anchors, or JSON-LD.
- Use the `impeccable` skill (`layout`) and the `good-css` skill. Scope is refine, not redesign.

## Handoff


`Sections.astro` groups a heading-led ContentSection with the following Features when it has no media and either has no items or links only to those Features' anchors. A plain Feature without media, bullets, a CTA, or a step label also introduces a following headingless Tabs block. This covers Sales Atlas without naming its route or heading. Adjacent `Krok n` Features form a visual group. If no intro H2 leads them, they retain their H2s.

Feature children under an intro use H3 and the existing Title role (28 px at 390, 35 px at 1440). The intro stays H2. Inner edges use the 16 px and 24 px spacing tokens. Outer edges retain the 48 px mobile and 64 px desktop section padding.
