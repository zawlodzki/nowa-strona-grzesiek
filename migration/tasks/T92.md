# T92. Homepage diagrams in the calm scene style (funnel, manual relay, AI agent)

## Goal

The animated diagrams still look like the Webflow template they came from: grey placeholder bars, English labels, a card with a border and a shadow around every element, five accent colours, bounce, shake and confetti. Grzesiek approved a calmer style on three homepage examples (2026-11-13). Rebuild those three scenes in it and give later scenes a shared base to build on.

## Approved direction

- Drawn with hairlines and type, no nested cards, shadows or placeholder bars. Real Polish labels and numbers.
- Colours only from the tokens, following DESIGN.md: Atlas Blue for the thing that moves and the result, Signal Cyan only as decoration for AI work (progress line, tag background), Error Red for delay and loss, Atlas Blue text on Cyan Tint. No orange, no green, no new colour tokens.
- Calm tempo: entrances about 630 ms with a 6 px rise, travel 1300–1440 ms, count-up 1620 ms, pauses 0.6–1.3 s, a scene runs 8–10 s. Curves `cubic-bezier(0.25, 1, 0.5, 1)` for entrances and `cubic-bezier(0.65, 0, 0.35, 1)` for travel. No bounce, shake, pulse or confetti. One thing moves at a time.
- The end state is in the HTML: complete without JavaScript and under `prefers-reduced-motion`. The scene plays once when it scrolls into view.
- Every scene ends on the same bar: a label on the left, one number in Erode on the right.
- The layout reflows to the slot in page-size text instead of scaling a fixed-width scene. Rendered labels stay at 12 px or more.

## Requirements

1. Shared base in `apps/web/src/components/diagrams/scene/`: one stylesheet on the site tokens (no local hex) and one timeline helper (play once on intersection, replay-safe cancellation, reduced motion leaves the end state).
2. Rebuild `crm-funnel`, `zw-manual-relay` and `zw-ai-sales` on that base. Keep each file's diagram key, `aria-hidden` and its place in `registry.ts` and `Diagram.astro`.
3. Keep `port-diagrams.mjs` from overwriting the rebuilt files.
4. The other 49 diagrams stay as they are.
5. Example company names, amounts and times are placeholders until Grzesiek confirms them.

## Done when

- [ ] No colour outside the site tokens; no text in Signal Cyan.
