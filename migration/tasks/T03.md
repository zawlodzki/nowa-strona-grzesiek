## Handoff

**What changed**

- `PRODUCT.md` (repo root). Written by `impeccable init` from the live site and the inventory, after one interview round with the user. The user confirmed three points: the audience (owners and sales leads at B2B companies of 2 to 500 people), the primary conversion (the free consultation, with the newsletter as the second path), and the positioning (process, CRM, automations and AI from one person). It contains no visual direction and no invented claims.
- `migration/lanes/design/out/audit.md`. It holds 27 ranked findings (P1 14, P2 11, P3 2), each with page, viewport and screenshot. It also has the Nielsen scores (marketing 22/36, blog 23/32), the technical audit score (11/20), the detector table and the systemic patterns.

**Deviations**

- Init step 5 (the build-path question and live-mode config) was skipped. This task audits a remote site and builds nothing.
- The critique ran dual-agent: a design review, and separately the detector plus a technical audit. They were then synthesised into one report.
- Not run: the `live-server` overlay injection and real touch gestures. The detector's URL mode drove its own headless Chrome.
- The interactive "what to fix next" questions from impeccable critique were not asked. The follow-up tasks own that.
