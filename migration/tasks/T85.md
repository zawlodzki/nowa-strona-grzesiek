# T85. Content sections: checklists, stat cards, paragraphs and tab panels

## Goal

Lists read as lists, stat cards follow DESIGN.md, and long copy has real paragraph spacing.

## Context

Visual audit of staging, 2026-11-13.

- **Checklists.** Items of "Skąd wiedzieć, że to dobry moment na współpracę?" (home), "Sprawdź, czy to wdrożenie Pipedrive pasuje do Twojej firmy" and "Czym Sales Atlas System nie jest" are `<h3>` in Erode 600 at 22.5 px. A list of four to six sentences set as headings is louder than the paragraphs around it and pollutes the outline.
- **Stat cards.** "Po wdrożeniu Pipedrive zostaje u Was" shows "1+ / 1 / 10+ / 1" as big numbers. DESIGN.md lists this as a don't ("count-up numbers for trivial deliverables"). The cards use a 6 px radius (boxed surfaces are 20 px) and centred text.
- **Paragraphs.** Body copy uses soft line breaks with no paragraph gap, so it reads as a wall: "Dobrze wdrożony Pipedrive to fundament, nie koniec" ("Pierwszy: … Drugi: … U klienta …"), the service rows on home, and `/o-mnie`, where bullet lists are typed as lines starting with "→".
- **Tabs (Sales Atlas layers).** The "Po czym poznać, że działa / nie działa" panels use a 6 px radius inside a 20 px panel, are flagged as nested cards by the detector, and their headings are `<h4>` directly under an `<h2>` (skipped level).

## Requirements

- Checklist items are list items in the Body role (Satoshi), optionally with medium weight for the lead phrase, not headings. Keep the check and ✕ icons and their colours.
- Stat cards: no oversized number when the value is a trivial count. Lead with the label (Title XS) and the description. Left-aligned, 20 px radius, padding from the spacing scale. If the layout stays data-driven by a `value` field, render it small or drop it when it is not a real metric, and say which rule you used.
- Text with line breaks renders as paragraphs with a visible gap (Body line-height plus a paragraph margin). Lines that start with "→" or "-" inside such text render as a real list. Do not edit Sanity content in this task. List content that should be retyped as rich text in Handoff.
- Tab panels: fix the heading level (H3), use one surface level (no card inside a card), radius per DESIGN.md (20 px for the boxed surface, 6 px only for parts inside it).
- Prose never runs past 68ch (DESIGN.md Wrap Rule).
- Do not touch `apps/web/src/components/diagrams/**`.
- Use the `impeccable` and `good-css` skills. Refine, not redesign.
