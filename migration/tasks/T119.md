# T119. Tabs semantics and find-in-page for hidden panels

## Goal

`Tabs.astro` starts as a list of anchor links to panel sections (a good no-JS baseline). Its script turns them into ARIA tabs. Two details differ from the `tabs` guide in the `modern-web-guidance` skill (`npx -y modern-web-guidance@latest retrieve "tabs"`):

- The script sets `aria-controls` on each tab. The guide says: "Do not add `aria-controls` to the tabs: no screen reader uses it."
- Inactive panels get plain `hidden`, so the browser's find-in-page cannot reach their text. The guide recommends `hidden="until-found"` with a `beforematch` handler that selects the matching tab.

Found in the modern-web-guidance audit of 2026-11-15.

## Requirements

1. Stop setting `aria-controls` on the tabs.
2. Hide inactive panels with `hidden="until-found"` and select the right tab in a `beforematch` handler, keeping the scroll-pinned desktop layout in sync. Where `until-found` is unsupported (feature-detect `'onbeforematch' in document.body`), keep plain `hidden`.
3. Follow the guide's layout cautions: `until-found` panels are not `display: none`, so no padding, border, margin or background on the panel element itself and no `gap` between panels. Desktop panels must look exactly as they do now; mobile follows requirement 7.
4. Keep the no-JS baseline (anchor list plus visible sections) and the keyboard behaviour.
5. Prove with `verify-web` at 375 px and 1280 px: desktop tabs look unchanged, mobile follows requirement 7, and in Chrome, Ctrl+F for text from a hidden panel opens its tab.
6. **Calm desktop switching.** Fix the scroll-pinned layer switching flash: no blink to empty, opacity flicker or layout jump. Follow emil-design-eng, improve-animations, review-animations and animation-performance. Record 1280 px before/after scroll frame sequences and reduced-motion behavior.
7. **Mobile accordion.** At 375 px expand each active panel directly below its own name, with other panels collapsed. Preserve the no-JS baseline, keyboard access, hidden="until-found" and find-in-page selection. Keep the tapped name in view. Capture live/new initial, layer 3 and layer 7 states with name-position probes.
