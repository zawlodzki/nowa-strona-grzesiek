# T110. Increase spacing between feature rows in grouped sections

## Goal

Give each feature heading, its copy, and its animation a clear boundary within the homepage services and Pipedrive steps.

## Done when

- [x] Consecutive feature rows have more space at 390 px and 1440 px on both pages.
- [x] The intro stays close to the first feature. Internal spacing and the gap after the group retain their values.
- [x] Both pages render through the local Worker with no horizontal overflow at 390 px.

## Handoff

The shared spacing for grouped rows follows the existing fluid section padding. It also applies to groups of consecutive steps without an intro.

Measured in Chrome against both local Worker builds. All homepage service rows and Pipedrive steps went from 48 px to 64 px at 390 px, 82.34 px at 992 px and 96 px at 1440 px. The intro gap stayed at 32 px. The text-to-media gap stayed at 24 px at 390 px and 48 px at wider test sizes. The gap to the next section stayed at 96 px, 114.34 px and 128 px, respectively.

Both routes returned 200. The 390 px viewports had a 390 px document scroll width. H3 IDs and alternating media order matched the baseline. Reviewed desktop and mobile screenshots of both routes. Impeccable reported zero findings.
