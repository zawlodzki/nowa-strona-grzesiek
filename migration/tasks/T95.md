# T95. Dedup the 15 contact forms and 6 newsletters into reusable sections

## Goal

T54 shipped `reusableSection` and `sectionRef` so a section can be edited once and attached to many pages. Existing pages were left alone: the same contact form is still inlined on 15 pages and the same newsletter on 6. This task replaces those duplicates with shared Sekcje documents and `sectionRef` blocks.

## Human gate

This writes to the **production** Sanity dataset (create Sekcje documents, patch pages, publish). It needs Grzesiek's explicit yes at the moment of the write, naming the dataset and the documents. Prepare everything as a dry-run or as drafts / a content release first, show him the plan (which documents, which pages, before/after), and wait. Do not write to production, and do not publish, without that yes. A yes to "add this task" or to merge T54 is not a yes to the write.

## Requirements

1. Inventory the 15 inlined contact forms and 6 inlined newsletters on the production dataset (read-only). Group them by identical content (including T53 copy fields). Record each group, the page paths, and any real differences, under `migration/lanes/cms/out/t95-dedup-sections/`. Do not assume every form or every newsletter is identical.
2. Propose one `reusableSection` (language-matched) per identical group, and a `sectionRef` replacement on each affected page, keeping the same `_key` or a recorded new key, and the same position in `page.sections`. Leave genuinely different sections inlined. Do not touch pages that already use `sectionRef`.
3. Show Grzesiek the plan (section names, which pages, what changes) and get his explicit yes before any production write.
4. After that yes: create the Sekcje documents, replace the inlined blocks with `sectionRef`, and publish. Verify on a production build (or `wrangler dev` reading the production dataset) that each affected page still renders the same form or newsletter. Save evidence in the out folder.
5. No schema or site-code change in this task (that is T54). No Studio deploy. No delete of unused documents without a separate yes.
