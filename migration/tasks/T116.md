# T116. Move allow-discrete out of the transition shorthand

## Goal

Five `transition` declarations put the `allow-discrete` keyword inside the shorthand: `Header.astro` (mobile nav, two declarations), `Faq.astro` (`::details-content`) and `SharePost.astro` (share popover, two declarations). The `navbar` guide in the `modern-web-guidance` skill says: "DO NOT put `allow-discrete` in the `transition` shorthand, which makes older browsers ignore the entire `transition` declaration". Declare `transition-behavior: allow-discrete` as a separate property after `transition` instead.

Found in the modern-web-guidance audit of 2026-11-15. Follow the `good-css` skill.

## Requirements

1. In each of the five declarations, remove `allow-discrete` from the shorthand and add `transition-behavior: allow-discrete;` right after the `transition` declaration in the same rule.
2. Keep durations, easings, the transitioned properties and the `prefers-reduced-motion` scoping as they are. No visual change in current Chrome, Safari and Firefox.
3. Use `verify-web` to prove that the mobile nav, FAQ items and share popover still animate open and closed (screenshots or a short trace at 375 px and 1280 px).

## Done when

- [ ] `grep -rnE "transition:[^;]*allow-discrete" apps/web/src` returns nothing.
- [ ] Every rule that transitions `display`, `overlay` or `content-visibility` has a separate `transition-behavior: allow-discrete`.
- [ ] The mobile nav, FAQ and share popover open and close the same way as before, with proof from `verify-web`.
- [ ] `pnpm --filter web build` and `tasks.py check` pass.
