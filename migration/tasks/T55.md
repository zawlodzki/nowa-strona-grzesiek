# T55. Blog post layout follow-up: wider reading column, no scrollbar in right column

## Goal

Grzesiek's feedback on staging after T48 (#67) on https://zawlodzki-web.zawlodzki.workers.dev/blog/automatyzacja-sprzedazy: the article text column is now narrower and harder to read, and the right column (CTA + related posts) has an unnecessary scrollbar. The same mechanism works well on https://aleksandraolesiewicz.com/blog/pcos-co-to-za-choroba/ — study that live page and replicate its column/sticky behaviour.

The fixes belong in the shared post template and must apply to every blog post, not only this one. Do not add per-post hacks.

Standing prefs: no cyan anywhere; Atlas Blue `#3a48cc` for accents. Do not change CTA copy.

## Current code (inspect 2026-11-12, post-T48 on main)

- Layout lives in `PostView.astro`. The header still uses `.content-grid` and `.post-head .inner` is limited to `--measure-prose` (68ch). `.post-layout` is now a centered box: `inline-size: min(100% - 2 * var(--layout-gutter), calc(var(--layout-container-max) - 2 * var(--layout-gutter)))`. `--layout-container-max` is 78rem, so at 1440px the three-column box is about 1200px wide. Columns are `12rem minmax(0, 1fr) 14rem` from 80rem up, with `--space-l` gaps. T48 evidence recorded the article (`.post-content`) at 720px at 1440. Before T48 the layout box was `min(100% - 2 * var(--space-m), 88rem)`, so aligning the TOC with the title/hero made the reading column narrower.
- Two columns (TOC + content) start at `min-width: 56rem`. Three columns (TOC / content / right CTA) start at `min-width: 80rem`. Below 80rem the sidebar CTA is `display: none` and related posts sit under the article. Keep that responsive order.
- Both side columns wrap their content in `.sticky { position: sticky; inset-block-start: 7rem; max-block-size: calc(100dvh - 8rem); overflow-y: auto; overscroll-behavior: contain; ... }`. That `max-block-size` + `overflow-y: auto` is the likely source of the unnecessary scrollbar in the right column (and can also appear on the TOC column).
- Related posts are still inlined in `PostView.astro` (`[data-related-posts]`). They stay `inert` / `aria-hidden` until the article midpoint crosses the viewport midpoint at `min-width: 80rem`, then animate with a 240ms ease-out on `opacity` and `transform` only; `prefers-reduced-motion` skips the motion. `RelatedPosts.astro` does not exist yet; it is reserved in `owns` if you extract one.
- Mobile TOC, single mobile CTA, share brand icons, and the mid-article related-posts motion were delivered in T48. Keep those gains.

## Requirements

1. **Study the reference first.** Open https://aleksandraolesiewicz.com/blog/pcos-co-to-za-choroba/ with the verify-web browser. Measure grid columns, the text column width in px and ch (the reading measure), how the side columns stick, what happens when a side column is taller than the viewport (there should be no inner scrollbar; content should scroll with the page), how related posts appear on scroll, and the breakpoints. Capture a 1440 reference screenshot. Replicate that mechanism in our post template as closely as fits our design.
2. **Wider reading column.** Give the article a comfortable measure that matches the reference text width as closely as our design allows, roughly 65–75ch. T48's 720px column at 1440 is too tight. Prefer a scoped fix in `PostView.astro`. Keep T48's alignment gain: the TOC must still line up with the title and hero, not start further left. If the only way to widen the article is also to widen the header/title measure, do that in `PostView.astro` and say so in the Handoff. Do not change global `layout.css` or tokens unless there is no other way; if that becomes necessary, add the file to `owns` in the PR and say why.
3. **No inner scrollbar in the right column.** Sticky TOC and right-column asides must not use `overflow: auto` / `overflow-y: auto` or a `max-height` / `max-block-size` that creates their own scrollbar. They may stay sticky. If the sticky content is taller than the viewport, it should scroll away with the page instead of scrolling inside the aside. Prove this on the right column at 1440 and 1280 (CTA + related posts visible after mid-article).
4. **Keep T48 gains.** Do not regress: title/hero alignment with the TOC; the right column disappears before the TOC when narrowing (3-col from 80rem, 2-col from 56rem is the current split — keep or improve, do not bring both side columns back at widths that squeeze the article); mobile TOC collapsible and collapsed-by-default, desktop side TOC stays expanded; a single CTA on mobile (hide the duplicate, do not edit `CtaBand.astro`); real X / LinkedIn / WhatsApp share icons and the T39 equal-tile grid; related posts reveal at about half the article with the existing Emil Kowalski-style animation (`transform` and `opacity` only, ~200–300ms ease-out, no layout shift) and instant show/hide when `prefers-reduced-motion` is set. CTA copy stays unchanged.
