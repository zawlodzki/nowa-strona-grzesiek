# T89. Footer groups, labels and heading semantics

## Goal

The footer is labelled truthfully and does not add fake H2s to every page.

## Context

Visual audit of staging, 2026-11-13.

- The column "Materiały" holds "O mnie", "Kontakt", "Blog", "System sprzedaży" and "Bezpieczne przesyłanie danych". Most of these are not materials.
- Column titles (CRM, AI, Asana, Materiały) are `<h2>` on all pages, so every page outline ends with four extra H2s.
- At 1440 px the Sales Atlas badge floats alone in the top-right corner, and the four columns fill only the left two thirds of the row.
- At 390 px the footer link "Blog" is 31 px wide (touch target below 44 px).

## Requirements

- Rename or regroup so each column label matches its links (for example "Firma" or "O mnie i kontakt" for the fourth column). If the labels come from Sanity settings, do not write to the dataset: propose the new labels in Handoff for the owner.
- Column titles are not H2. Use a `<nav aria-label>` per group with a non-heading title, or a lower heading level that keeps the outline valid.
- Place the Sales Atlas badge deliberately (next to the logo, or as the lead of a column). Columns use the full container width.
- Every footer link has a tap area of at least 44 × 44 px at 390 px.
- Keep the "Zarządzaj zgodami" control wired to the consent manager, and keep all link URLs unchanged.
- Use the `impeccable` and `good-css` skills.

## Done when

- [ ] Before/after footer screenshots at 390 and 1440 px.
- [ ] Heading outline of `/` ends at the last content H2, with no footer H2s.
- [ ] Smallest footer tap target measured at 390 px is ≥ 44 × 44 px.
- [ ] A crawl diff shows the same set of footer link URLs.
- [ ] `pnpm test`, the build and the e2e suite pass.
