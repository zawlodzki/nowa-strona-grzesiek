# T18. Routes: Sanity data to pages

## Goal

Every kept URL renders from Sanity with the block components and the SEO head.

## Inputs

- migration/contracts/url-map.csv

## Steps

1. Static paths from Sanity per language; PL without prefix, EN under `/en/`.
2. Blog index with real pagination (`/blog?page=` not indexed) and post template.

## Done when

- [ ] Every keep row in url-map.csv returns 200 on staging. Passes locally through the Worker on the `development` dataset (108/108). Not yet on staging: production is empty until the importers run there (T20).

## Handoff

**What changed.**
- `apps/web/src/lib/sanity/` is the Sanity layer. `client.ts` runs GROQ against the CDN API. The dataset comes from `SANITY_DATASET` and defaults to `production`. `site.ts` loads every page, post, image asset, logo, testimonial, service, the person and the translation groups in one query per build. It parses them once at the boundary and throws on a missing language, slug, title or SEO title or description. `routes.ts` is the route table. Every URL the site serves comes from `buildRoutes()`, and it throws when two documents claim one URL. `seo.ts` maps a route to T12's `SeoPage`. `Sections.astro` maps `page.sections` to the T09 and T10 block components and resolves their references. `RouteView.astro`, `PostView.astro` and `BlogIndexView.astro` render the three route kinds.
- Route files only pick their slice of the table. `index.astro` takes the PL home. `blog/index.astro` takes the first blog page and `blog/[slug].astro` the PL posts. `[...slug].astro` takes the other PL pages and blog pages 2 and up. `en/[...slug].astro` takes every EN route and is empty until EN documents exist.
- `Base.astro` takes either `seo: SeoPage`, which renders `<Seo>` with the one `<title>` and sets `<html lang>`, or the old bare `title`, `description` and `noindex` for utility pages.
- `pages/sitemap/_content.ts` now reads through `lib/sanity/client.ts`. The sitemap, RSS and llms.txt therefore follow the same dataset as the pages.

**Deviations.**
- The owner approved extending `owns` to `Base.astro`, `pages/index.astro` and `pages/sitemap/_content.ts` on 2026-10-21. T12 asked for the head slot, `index.astro` takes priority over `[...slug]` for `/`, and T13 asked T18 to take over `_content.ts`.
- `index.astro` keeps the noindex placeholder until a PL page with slug `/` is published. Without it, `/` would answer 404 on an empty `production`.
- Pagination is path-based at `/blog/strona/<n>`, 12 posts per page, noindex, linked with `rel=prev` and `rel=next`. `/blog?page=<n>` would need the Worker (T13) to map the query to a static file. The static build ignores the query, so `/blog?page=2` serves page 1, with canonical `/blog`. If query-based pagination matters, that is a Worker change.
- `_content.ts` keeps its own discovery query and parser. Only the client and dataset are shared. Merging it into `site.ts` would also touch the sitemap, RSS and llms routes and their test, which this task does not own.
- An internal link to a document that is not published renders as plain text, with a build warning. Weak references may dangle by design, and T10's `Span` throws on an unresolved link.
- A page whose first section is `richText` (the legal pages) gets its title as a visible H1, as on the live site.
- Page kind: `/` is `home`, `/kontakt` is `contact`, `/o-mnie` is `about`, and a page referenced by a `service` document is `service`, with offers taken from its pricing plans. Everything else is `generic`. Course pages render as `service` or `generic`, because the schema has no course fields.

**Follow-ups.**
- T16: on `development`, `/404`, `/podziekowanie-newsletter` and `/podziekowanie-konsultacja` have no `seo.description`, which the schema requires. The build stops on them. The live pages have no description, so either the importer fills one in or the schema makes it optional for noindex pages. That second option also needs `Seo.description` to be optional in `seo/types.ts` and `meta.ts` (T12).
- T16: `development` has 0 `service` documents, so service pages emit `WebPage` without a `Service` node.
- T09: `/o-mnie` opens with a `feature` whose heading is the live H1. `Feature` accepts only `h2` or `h3`. Allow `headingLevel="h1"` and the route passes it for a leading feature. `/newsletter` and `/darmowe-materialy-dla-biznesow-uslugowych` have no H1 on the live site either.
- verify-web: `doctor` checks for `<title>Grzegorz Zawłodzki</title>`. A Sanity home has its real SEO title, so the check fails on a healthy instance. Match the site name inside the title instead.
- No header, navigation or footer is rendered yet. `siteSettings.nav` and `footer` exist in the schema but no task owns the components.
- Images use the original asset capped at 1600 px with `auto=format`. There is no `srcset` yet.
