# T100. Asana training page diagrams in the calm scene style (problem and steps 1–5)

## Goal

Grzesiek asked to continue with the Asana pages, then with the subpages after them. `/szkolenie-i-wdrozenie-asana` has six scenes: the problem, which is shared with `/audyt-procesow-asana`, and one scene for each of the five steps. Rebuild them in the calm style.

## Context

- This task builds on T96–T99 (PRs #107, #108, #109, #112), all in review. Its branch starts from the T99 branch, so merge those PRs first.
- Most of these scenes are lists in which every row gets a status. To avoid copying the same CSS into every scene, add a reusable `ListScene.astro` on `sequence.ts`. The audit page (next task) will reuse it.
- Page copy:
  - Problem: project information lives in mail and in people's heads, the status has to be rebuilt in meetings, and the team fears extra reporting.
  - Step 1: pick the first repeatable processes with real value, not the whole company.
  - Step 2: build templates, roles, deadlines and rules in workshops.
  - Step 3: run real projects, and simplify or remove whatever the team does not use.
  - Step 4: training on good habits: inbox instead of e-mail, My tasks, discussion in the task instead of a messenger, what not to put in Asana.
  - Step 5: hand the system over to a leader who owns the admin, the documentation and the onboarding pattern.

## Requirements

1. `ListScene.astro`: rows with a name, an optional "before" value that gives way to the "after" value (or a pending mark), tones (ok, loss, muted, struck), an optional to-scale bar, and the closing number computed from the rows that count.
2. Rebuild the six scenes. Use `ListScene` where it fits, and `sequence.ts` throughout with no new scene scripts. Follow the approved direction recorded in T92, and carry the T96 header marker.
3. `zw-asana-problem` must read for both Asana pages.
4. Names, numbers and dates are placeholders until Grzesiek confirms them.

### What changed

- `ListScene.astro` (new): the reusable list scene on `sequence.ts`. Each row has a name, a pending mark that gives way to its value, or a "before" value that is struck through with "→ after" beside it. Rows take tones (`ok` Atlas Blue, `loss` Error Red, `muted`), an optional pill, an optional struck name and an optional bar drawn to scale. The header is either a titled head (Erode title plus subtitle) or two column labels. The closing number counts the rows marked `counts`, with an optional "z N". Under 26rem each row stacks.
- Six scenes, each a thin wrapper over `ListScene` with the T96 header marker:
  - `zw-asana-problem` (both Asana pages): "Projekt Acme — gdzie są ustalenia?" Scope is in the sales e-mail, the deadline in the lead's head, the status rebuilt at the meeting and the files on a private drive. Only the tasks are in Asana. It closes on "Ustalenia zapisane w Asanie · 1 z 5" in red.
  - `zw-asana-process` (step 1): five processes with monthly runs drawn to scale (max 12). Two go "na start" and the rest "później". "2 z 5".
  - `zw-asana-template` (step 2): the stages of a client-onboarding template each get an owner and a day. "4 z 4".
  - `zw-asana-real-work` (step 3): template elements with their usage after four weeks, drawn to scale. Elements used less than 50% are struck through and marked "usuwamy". "Usunięte z szablonu · 3 z 6".
