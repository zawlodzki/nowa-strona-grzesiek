# T86. One button system and a Stripe-style hover arrow on CTAs

## Goal

Every button on the site comes from one size system, and CTAs end in a chevron ">" that extends into an arrow "→" on hover, as on stripe.com.

## Context

### Inconsistencies (visual audit of staging, 2026-11-13, 1440 px)

|---|---|---|---|---|
| Header "Bezpłatna konsultacja" | 51 | 8/16 | 999 | 500 |
| Hero and section CTAs | 46 | 8/24 | 999 | 500 |
| Pricing card buttons | 51 | 8/24 | 999 | 500 |
| "Następne wpisy" (blog) | 52 | 8/24 | 999 | 500 |
| Newsletter "Zapisuję się" | 57 | 8/24 | 999 | 400 |
| Form submit "Wyślij zapytanie" | 51 | 8/24 | 50 px | 400 |

- The CTA band button stretches to about 464 px wide, and on `/sales-atlas-system`, `/kurs-ai-w-sprzedazy` and `/wdrozenie-ai-w-sprzedazy-b2b` its label wraps to two lines.
- "Zapisuję się" on the navy band still has the cyan ring. DESIGN.md: the on-dark button has no cyan ring.
- The newsletter heading clips a gradient to the word "Atlas" (`.accent`, `background-clip: text`) and the band is `linear-gradient(45deg, …)` behind text. DESIGN.md bans gradient text and asks for a solid Night Navy behind text. "Atlas" becomes solid Signal Cyan on navy (9.90:1).
- The form submit button lives in `LeadForm.astro` and is aligned in T87, which depends on this task. Export whatever T87 needs to reuse (class or component) and describe it in Handoff.

### How the Stripe arrow works (inspected on stripe.com, 2026-11-13)

Markup: the label text is followed by a small inline SVG, `width="5" height="8" viewBox="0 0.5 5 8"`, with `margin-left: 6px`. Inside it:

```html
<svg class="hds-icon hds-icon-hover-arrow" width="5" height="8" fill="none" viewBox="0 0.5 5 8">
  <defs><clipPath id="…"><rect x="0" y="0" width="12" height="9"/></clipPath></defs>
  <g clip-path="url(#…)">
    <g class="arrow-group">
      <rect class="shaft" x="-10" y="3.375" width="13" height="1.75" fill="currentColor"/>
      <path d="M4.84766 3.63379L5.45898 4.25 … Z" fill="currentColor"/>  <!-- the chevron -->
    </g>
  </g>
</svg>
```

- At rest: `.shaft` has `opacity: 0`, `.arrow-group` has no transform. Only the chevron shows.
- On hover of the link: `.arrow-group` gets `transform: translateX(6px)` and `.shaft` gets `opacity: 1`. The shaft is drawn to the left of the chevron (from x = −10) and the clip rect starts at x = 0, so as the group slides right the shaft appears to grow out of the label side while the chevron tip moves forward.
- Timing: `transform 0.3s cubic-bezier(0.25, 1, 0.5, 1)` on the group and `opacity 0.3s cubic-bezier(0.25, 1, 0.5, 1)` on the shaft.
- The SVG box stays 5 px wide. The arrow extends into reserved space, so the label and the button width never shift.

Reproduce the behaviour, not Stripe's code or path data. Draw our own chevron and shaft in the stroke style of the existing `Icon.astro` arrow (stroke 1.8, round caps).

## Requirements

### Arrow
- `Button.astro` (all variants, including `text` and `on-dark`) ends with the new chevron. On `:hover` and `:focus-visible` it extends to the arrow: the head moves forward and the shaft fades or draws in. Only `transform` and `opacity` animate.
- No layout shift: the button's width and the label position are identical at rest and on hover. Reserve the travel space inside the icon box.
- Trigger is the whole button, inside `@media (hover: hover) and (pointer: fine)`. On touch the chevron stays as is.
- `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce`: no movement. Showing the arrow state without a transition is fine.
- Duration and easing come from the motion tokens in `tokens.css` (about 300 ms, ease-out, no bounce or overshoot). If no token fits, write a `Contract request` for `tokens.json` in Handoff and use the closest existing token.
- The icon stays `aria-hidden="true"`. The accessible name is the label only.
- Other `Icon` uses (`check`, `close`) are unchanged.
- Use the `animate` and `animation-performance` skills for the motion and `good-css` for the CSS.

### Button system
- One height (44 px minimum, one value at each breakpoint), one padding, radius 999 px and weight 500 everywhere, including the header CTA, pricing buttons, "Następne wpisy" and the newsletter button.
- The CTA band button sizes to its label (no stretching) and stays on one line at ≥ 768 px. When the label is too long for the row, the band stacks heading above button instead of wrapping the label.
- On-dark buttons have no cyan ring.
- Newsletter band: solid Night Navy behind the text, "Atlas" in solid Signal Cyan, no `background-clip: text`.
- Full width below 480 px stays (DESIGN.md).
